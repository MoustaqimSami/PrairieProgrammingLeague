## What is PPL?

The Prairie Programming League is a monthly programming contest for high school and post-secondary students across the prairies. Contests are mostly in person, at participating universities, and run on [Kattis](https://open.kattis.com).

## How each contest works

Every contest day has two parts:

| | When | Internet | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **In-person contest** | Saturday, 11:00 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. | Not allowed | Coaches may help with the two easiest problems |
| **Upsolve contest** | Saturday 1:30 p.m. to noon the next Friday | Allowed | Your code must be your own |

The problems are meant to be approachable: usually 6 to 7 per contest, with about 4 at Kattis difficulty 3 or lower. That's a guideline, not a promise.

## Who can compete

Compete on your own or as a team of 2 or 3. Problems are chosen with individual competitors in mind, so going solo is a perfectly good way to play. See the [rules](/rules) for eligibility.

## Prizes

There's a **$1,000 prize pool** across the season, given out by prize draws after the final contest in April. You earn draw tickets by showing up and solving problems. See [how prizes work](/prizes).
