## About the league

The Prairie Programming League is a series of team programming contests for university and high school students. Each round is a few hours of algorithmic problems on [Kattis](https://open.kattis.com), and results count toward season standings.

**Who can join:** teams of up to three students from the same school. Beginners and experienced competitors are both welcome.

**Why join:** practice for the ICPC and other contests, meet programmers from across the prairies, and have fun solving problems.
