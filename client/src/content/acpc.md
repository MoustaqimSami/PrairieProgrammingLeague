# ACPC

The **Alberta Collegiate Programming Contest (ACPC)** is Alberta's big annual university programming contest. PPL is good practice for it.

## About the contest

- Held **every fall semester**. The University of Calgary has hosted it since 2011, and the University of Alberta hosted it from 2003 to 2010.
- Run by the University of Calgary's Competitive Programming Club and Problem Solving Club, with support from Arcurve and the UCalgary Department of Computer Science.
- Teams spend **five hours** solving programming problems that test problem-solving, programming, math and teamwork. Teams are ranked by the number of problems solved.
- Results are split into **Division I** and **Division II**.
- The 2025 contest was on October 18, 2025. Registration has a deadline, and capacity is limited: past contests have sold out, so register early.
